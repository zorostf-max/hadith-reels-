TITLE: GTA 6 reportedly hit 20M pre-orders! GameStop CEO & Crimson Desert #shorts
---
GTA 6: analysts estimate it reached around 20 million pre-orders before launch (an estimate, not an official Rockstar/Take-Two number) (IGN).
GameStop CEO Ryan Cohen says the move to digital-only games like disc-less GTA 6 hurts consumers, who should be able to sell or trade what they buy (GamesRadar+, IGN).
After its Game Awards snub, Crimson Desert won a record 8 categories at the Korea Game Awards (GamesRadar+).
