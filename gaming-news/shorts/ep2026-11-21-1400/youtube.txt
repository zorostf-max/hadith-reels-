GTA VI: The Festival takes place today, November 21, at Mana Wynwood in Miami, partly presented by PlayStation, with Travis Scott, Cardi B, Metro Boomin and more (Push Square, The FADER).

Bethesda confirms A Wasteland Full of Cats, "a Fallout-inspired adventure" by Devcats, alongside a campaign with animal rescue Dallas Pets Alive (PC Gamer).
