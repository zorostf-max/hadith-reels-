TITLE: GTA 6 vs Zelda: a Metacritic review war! GTA 6 on Switch 2 & a Black Friday deal #shorts
---
GTA 6 and the Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake are caught in a Metacritic review bomb war: GTA 6's PS5 user score fell to 5.0, and the remake's dropped from 9.6 to 9.0 after a wave of zero scores (Dexerto).
Digital Foundry says a Switch 2 version of GTA 6 "feels extremely unlikely": the game is so CPU-heavy it is limited to 30fps even on PS5 Pro (Nintendo Insider).
Nintendo's Black Friday offer: the Switch 2 + Mario Kart World bundle drops to $499.99 in the US, November 22 to December 5 (Screen Rant).
