Rockstar Games staff voted against recognising the Rockstar Game Workers Union: 93.2% turnout, 75% against, 25% in favour (Eurogamer).

Ubisoft says "this doesn't mean that projects are required to use generative AI," after Vertical Slice reported teams had to justify not using it (GamesRadar+).
