TITLE: GTA 6 broke Comcast's traffic record! Rockstar's veterans & Persona news #shorts
---
GTA 6: Comcast says November 18, the final preload day, was the busiest traffic day in its history, with peak traffic 31% above an average day (GameSpot, IGN).
Rockstar confirms about 1,000 employees, around 21% of its staff, have worked there for at least 10 years (GamesRadar+, PC Gamer).
Atlus promises future Persona games won't take as long as they used to, while Persona 6 is still in development (Eurogamer).
