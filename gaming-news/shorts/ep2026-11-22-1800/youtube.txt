TITLE: GTA 6 at 60fps on Xbox?! A 64GB VRAM GPU & AI games vs the '80s crash #shorts

An Xbox Series X glitch can reportedly run GTA 6 at 60fps, but animations may play at double speed (GamesRadar+).
Moore Threads teased its Lushan gaming GPU; a product manager claims it could pack 64GB of VRAM, double the RTX 5090's 32GB (Tom's Hardware).
