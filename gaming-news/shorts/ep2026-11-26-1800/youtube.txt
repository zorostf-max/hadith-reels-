TITLE: Take-Two wiped 3,007 GTA 6 PC port repos! Rockstar's cut tennis & a AAAA lawsuit #shorts
---
📌 Follow for gaming news 4× a day 🎮
VideoCardz reports Take-Two issued a DMCA takedown on 3,007 GitHub repositories for unofficial GTA 6 PC ports. There's still no official PC version.
Rockstar leads Rob Nelson and Scott Mitchell told PCGames that tennis, crafting, Sims-style home decorating and movie theater visits were cut from GTA 6.
Ubisoft argued in a lawsuit that calling Skull and Bones a AAAA game was "classic puffery"; a judge sided with Ubisoft in August (via Game File).
