# Instagram growth plan — @hadithdaily.bukhari (2026-11-13)

Audit: 9 reels, 1–202 plays. Recent hadith reels got 1–12 plays (not pushed to non-followers).
Likely causes: blank cover frames (fixed: hook scene visible from frame 0 + cover.jpg), 7–8 hashtags
per caption (Instagram caps at 5 since Dec 2025 — over the cap reduces reach), almost no followers to seed.

Competitor outliers (Arabic Islamic reels, Aug–Nov 2026): 0.7–1.7M views each.
- Hook spoken/shown in second 0, story framed as a question («شو اللي خلّى النبي ﷺ…»), cliffhanger "part 2".
- Share-driven CTA (sends are Instagram's strongest signal): «انشرها تؤجر».
- Parents angle is huge for prophets'/companions' stories for kids → our seerah + stories series.
- Talking-head or faceless text both work; topics: love of the Companions, the Prophet's ﷺ mercy, duas with a stated reward, "5 hadiths that help your life".

Automated (runbooks):
1. Max 3–5 hashtags on every caption (README, seerah, stories).
2. Hook first line + 💬 question + share CTA (already in place).
3. Seerah/stories captions add «👨‍👩‍👧 شاهدها مع أطفالك وشاركها مع كل أب وأم».
4. Slots 10:00 and 18:00 Istanbul stay: Metricool best-time data peaks 10:00–12:00 and 17:00–18:00.
